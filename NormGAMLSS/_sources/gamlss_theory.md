# GAMLSS Theory and Concepts

## What is GAMLSS?

Generalized Additive Models for Location, Scale, and Shape (GAMLSS) extend traditional regression by modeling not just the mean (location), but also:

- **Location (μ)**: The mean of the distribution
- **Scale (σ)**: The variance/spread of the distribution  
- **Shape parameters**: Skewness (ν) and kurtosis (τ)

## Key Advantages

1. **Flexible distributions**: Choose from 100+ distributions beyond normal
2. **Non-parametric smoothing**: Model non-linear relationships
3. **All parameters modeled**: Not just the mean, but variance and shape too
4. **Centile estimation**: Directly calculate percentiles and z-scores

## When to Use GAMLSS

- When your data doesn't follow normal distribution
- When you need to model changing variance
- For centile/percentile analysis
- When dealing with skewed or heavy-tailed data
